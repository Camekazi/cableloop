Why It Matters

Every cable counts

  • Cables contain copper, aluminum, and plastic that can be recovered and reused
  • E-waste is the fastest growing waste stream globally, growing by 2M tonnes annually
  • Improper disposal leaches heavy metals into soil and water supplies
  • One recycled cable can provide materials for manufacturing new electronics
